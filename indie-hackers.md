# Indie Hackers templates

## 1) Build in public — soft launch

**Title:** I shipped a tiny [CATEGORY] product for [AUDIENCE] — here's what I learned in week 1

```
Hook (2–3 lines): the painful moment that made you build this.

What I shipped:
- One-sentence what it is
- Who it's for
- Price (be honest)

Week 1 numbers (even if small):
- Visitors:
- Sales:
- Unexpected learning:

One tactical tip others can steal (no link required).

Link (optional, end): [product]

What I'd love feedback on: _____
```
